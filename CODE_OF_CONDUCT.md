# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Participants will maintain a respectful, inclusive, evidence-focused research environment. Critique should address claims, methods, assumptions, code, or artifacts rather than personal characteristics.

## Expected conduct

- identify uncertainty and mistakes without ridicule;
- credit sources, contributors, and affected communities;
- welcome questions about mathematics, software, and terminology;
- distinguish disagreement from misconduct;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, threatening, or exploitative behavior;
- protect private, sensitive, and security-relevant information.

## Research conduct

Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, citation laundering, hidden threshold changes, misleading visual encodings, and unsupported claims of validation are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project steward. Responses may include clarification, correction, warning, removal of material, restricted participation, or referral to the relevant platform or institution. Decisions and remedies should be proportionate, documented, and attentive to reporter safety.
