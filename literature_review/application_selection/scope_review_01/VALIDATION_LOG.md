## Source-verification procedure

Each curated anchor was checked against at least one of the following: publisher metadata, DOI record, PubMed or PubMed Central, National Academies Press, OSTI or national-laboratory record, or an institutional repository. Records without a verified DOI use a persistent publisher or institutional URL and explicitly state the limitation. The focal `Design for Descent` paper retains its separate full-paper validation record.

The citation audit corrected bibliographic fields rather than merely restyling them. Material corrections include the title of the power-flow paper (A010); the exact titles of the drainage and water-network papers (A014–A015, A017); the DOI and full title of the waste review (A023); the year, DOI, volume, issue, and pages of the food-system paper (A029); the final issue year, title, volume, issue, and pages of the humanitarian-supply review (A030); the year and DOI of the infrastructure-restoration paper (A032); the published DOI and pagination of the atmospheric-river paper (A038); and the final publication year of the National Academies report (A042). The 2026 correction to A031 is recorded without replacing the original article DOI.

The review does not claim that all 42 papers were independently reproduced, critically appraised at full-text level, or assessed for risk of bias. Those activities belong to the next review phase.
