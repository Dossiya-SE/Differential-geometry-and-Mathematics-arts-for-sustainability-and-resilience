# Scientific mind maps — taxonomy-first baseline

The [mathematics taxonomy](mathematics_taxonomy.svg) contains one root, three branches and nine leaves. Its lines mean **parent–child membership only**; they have no arrowheads and make no empirical, operational or causal claim. The example is nonexhaustive, proposed, and application-neutral (`MSR-FIG-0006`).

Adaptation: confirm root, unique concept IDs, one parent per nonroot concept, acyclicity, complete labels and semantic category consistency before drawing. Never infer a dependency merely because nodes share a branch. The [v1 standard](../../docs/VISUAL_SYSTEM_V1.md) governs layouts and review.
